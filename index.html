ScamShield AI uses a deterministic heuristic matching engine running entirely in your browser. It scans text against weighted cyber threat patterns:

  • Urgency & Threats (Weight 25): High-pressure words designed to induce panic (e.g., account blocked, within 24 hours).
  • OTP & Credential Requests (Weight 30): Attempts to harvest secret authentication tokens, PINs, or passwords.
  • Suspicious Rewards & Offers (Weight 20): Unsolicited lotteries, free prizes, or cashback hooks.
  • Financial Pressure (Weight 25): Demands for immediate payment, processing fees, or money transfers.
  • Suspicious Links (Weight 15): Unverified URLs or shortened links embedded in messages.
  • Impersonation Signals (Weight 20): Authority impersonation (bank support, KYC update) combined with sensitive requests.

Scores are capped at 100. Repeated instances of the same category do not inflate the score. Low scores do not guarantee safety, and high scores do not independently prove criminal intent.

Recommended Actions

ScamShield AI's URL Analyzer uses a deterministic offline heuristic inspection engine to evaluate link structures:

  • IP-Address Hostnames (Weight 25): Direct numeric IP links often bypass typical domain registration transparency.
  • Embedded User Info (Weight 25): `@` symbols in URLs can mask the actual destination domain.
  • Suspicious Keywords (Weight 20): Words like 'login', 'verify', 'secure', 'account' in unconventional path contexts.
  • Excessive Subdomains (Weight 20): Multiple nested subdomains designed to imitate trusted brands.
  • Non-Standard Ports (Weight 20): Ports outside standard web traffic (80/443) used in unexpected services.
  • Shortened Links (Weight 15): Obfuscated redirect links where the destination isn't immediately visible.
  • HTTP Protocol (Weight 15): Unencrypted connections lacking TLS security.
  • Punycode / Internationalized Domain (Weight 15): 'xn--' prefixes that can facilitate homograph spoofing.

Scores are capped at 100. Legitimate sites may trigger some heuristic warnings, and sophisticated phishing URLs can appear clean. Always verify destinations independently.

Improvement Recommendations

ScamShield AI's Password Strength Checker evaluates passwords against deterministic security principles:

  • Length & Passphrases: Longer inputs exponentially increase guessing complexity. Using multi-word passphrases provides strong security.
  • Character Variety: Mixing uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols increases the keyspace.
  • Pattern Detection: Identifies predictable sequences (e.g., `123456`, `qwerty`), repeated characters (`aaaaaa`), and common weak phrases.
  • Whitespace Awareness: Checks for leading or trailing whitespace, which can cause login failures or accidental typing errors.

Scores are capped at 100. This is a local educational helper and does not replace cryptographic strength evaluation or enterprise vault checks.
