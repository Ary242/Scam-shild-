    • OTP & Credential Requests (Weight 30): Attempts to harvest secret authentication tokens, PINs, or passwords.

              Common Emergency Scenarios

              Immediate Action: Contact your bank or payment app support immediately to block your UPI handle, debit card, or account.

              Reporting: Call 1930 promptly to report financial fraud and file a formal report on cybercrime.gov.in.

              Preserve all transaction references, UTR numbers, SMS alerts, and screenshots.

              Immediate Action: Use the service's official recovery process (such as password reset links or account recovery forms) to regain control.

              Security Upgrade: Change compromised credentials immediately and enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) using an authenticator app.

              Check active sessions and log out any unrecognized devices or browsers.

              Immediate Action: Secure affected accounts where identification documents or personal data were shared.

              Monitoring: Watch bank statements, credit reports, and social media accounts closely for unauthorized activity or loans taken in your name.

              Avoid sharing sensitive IDs on unverified websites or with unverified callers.

              Critical Warning: Scammers frequently monitor online forums or social media and pose as "recovery agents" or cyber police promising to recover lost funds for a fee.

              Protection: Never pay advance fees, share OTPs, or install remote-access software (AnyDesk, TeamViewer) with anyone claiming they can recover your money.

              Only official law enforcement and banking authorities investigate financial fraud.

              Immediate Action: Do not click links, reply to, or call numbers listed in suspicious messages.

              Access: Access official services independently by typing web addresses or opening verified mobile applications.

              Use ScamShield AI's Message Scanner and URL Analyzer to check future messages safely.

              Emergency Safety Notice: Act promptly, contact the relevant official service, and never share your OTP, PIN, password, or remote-access code with someone claiming to help.               Step 7 Module

              Cybersecurity Demo Lab

              Explore how common online scams work through safe, fictional demonstrations.

              Educational Demo Lab: All demonstrations use safe, fictional examples. No real-world security scans or external API requests are performed.

              Demo 1: Spot the Scam Message

              Message Analysis Demo

              Examine a fictional scam message and discover its warning signs.

              "URGENT: Your account will be suspended today! Share your OTP immediately to avoid losing access."

              Educational Breakdown

              • 1
                Urgency Pressure: "URGENT... suspended today!" uses high-pressure language to induce panic and prevent careful thinking.
              • 2
                OTP Request: "Share your OTP immediately" attempts to harvest secret authentication tokens to authorize unauthorized access.
              • 3
                Legitimate Services Rule: Legitimate banks and services never ask users to disclose their OTPs or passwords over messages or phone calls.
              • 4
                Safety Recommendation: Ignore the message, do not reply or call listed numbers, and verify account status through official mobile apps.

              Demo 2: Understand a Suspicious URL

              URL Inspection Demo

              Inspect a fictional demonstration URL structure safely without opening it.

              http://192.0.2.1/login/verify

              Educational Breakdown

              • 1
                HTTP Protocol: Uses unencrypted HTTP rather than secure HTTPS, leaving data vulnerable in transit.
              • 2
                IP Address Hostname: Uses a numeric IP address (`192.0.2.1`, a reserved documentation IP) instead of a recognized domain name.
              • 3
                Sensitive Keywords: The path contains `/login/verify`, commonly used in phishing pages mimicking login portals.
              • 4
                Caution Signal: These structural traits are caution signals and warning indicators, not definitive proof that the site is malicious. Never open unfamiliar test links.

              Demo 3: Password Safety Learning

              Password Strength Demo

              Compare a weak password against a strong passphrase to learn security principles.

              Fictional Example B River-Candle-Cloud-Train-42!

              Educational Breakdown

              • 1
                Length & Passphrases: Longer passphrases (Example B) provide significantly higher resistance against automated cracking than short predictable words (Example A).
